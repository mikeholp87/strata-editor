This is a browser demo with sample files. It is a simplified model of the native Qt app. Edits stay in this browser tab (and your local storage) and never leave your computer.

Try a Strata workspace

Edit the Markdown on the left and watch the preview follow. Open other files from the explorer, switch between Source, Split and Preview, or press Ctrl+Shift+P for commands.
